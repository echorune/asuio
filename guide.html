What’s it for?

  1. Choose Send money on the overview and enter a recipient and amount.
  2. Amounts must be greater than £0, use up to two decimal places, and fit within the available balance. A reference is optional.
  3. Select Review transfer, check the recipient, source account, note, and amount, then confirm. Edit details returns to the form.
  4. The demo transfer appears in activity and reduces the displayed demo balance.
Transfers are simulated locally. No real payment is sent.

Holding details show position value, units held, average cost, asset class, sector, and risk profile. Watchlist-only assets show market price and snapshot change. The detail action can add or remove an asset from the watchlist; it never places an order.

The whole application is a prototype. Account activity, account details, prices, and portfolio returns are sample data and are not connected to a bank or brokerage.
